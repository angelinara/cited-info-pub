Last updated 15 September 2026

cited.info aims to give general information about consumer law. It is not legal advice, and it cannot take account of your particular circumstances. The information may be inaccurate or out of date, so please verify it against official legislation and case law.

What the pages contain

Each topic attempts to quote the relevant legislation from official sources, with added links. Where case law may apply, the topic names the case, summarises the relevant principles, and links to the judgment. Alongside the quotes is a plain-English reading of the substance. The readings and case summaries are ours, and they can be wrong or incomplete.

Where the law applies

Topics aim to describe the law of England and Wales. In some areas, such as renting, the law in Wales differs from England, so check that a topic applies to where you live. The law in Scotland and Northern Ireland can also differ.

Search results

A search match suggests a topic that may be relevant. If a search does not match a topic, the site may not cover it yet, or the search may not have found it. In either case, the absence of a result does not indicate whether you have legal rights.

No solicitor-client relationship

cited.info is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or representation. Use of the site does not create a solicitor-client relationship.

Getting help

For advice on your situation, contact Citizens Advice. To find a solicitor, use the Law Society's directory. Official guidance is available on GOV.UK.
